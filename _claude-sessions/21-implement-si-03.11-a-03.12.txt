● SI-03.12 is done: the worker now runs a maintenance job every hour, and all 17
  tests for this step pass. The type-check and lint are clean.

  What the job does:
  - Abandoned drafts (older than UPLOAD_ABANDON_AFTER_HOURS, default 24 h): the
    video goes to failed with reason upload_abandoned. The unfinished upload is
    cancelled, its files in storage are deleted, and the row is kept. If an
    upload completes at the same moment, the completion wins.
  - Videos stuck in processing (longer than PROCESSING_STUCK_AFTER_MINUTES,
    default 120 min):
    - no job in the queue → it is queued again;
    - its job already failed → the video goes to failed with reason
      processing_failed;
    - its job is still waiting or running → nothing changes.
  - Restarting the worker keeps a single hourly schedule; it doesn't create
    duplicates.

  Two choices of mine you may want to review:
  - If cleaning up one video throws an error, the job logs it and moves on to
    the next video. Otherwise one bad video would block the rest every hour.
  - The plan marks a draft as failed before deleting its files. If the deletion
    then fails, the next run won't retry it, because the draft is no longer a
    draft. Leftover upload parts are discarded by the storage server after 24 h.
    Files already saved under videos/{id}/ stay.

  Once the running video-worker reloads, it creates the hourly schedule on its
  own. All notes are in docs/phases/phase-03-videos/progress.md.
